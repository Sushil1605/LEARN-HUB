Help & Support

How do I enroll in a course?

Click on any course card and select "Enroll Now". You'll need to be logged in to enroll.

Can I get a certificate?

Yes! Complete 100% of a course to earn a digital certificate.

How long does a course take?

Most courses take 4-12 weeks depending on your pace and the course level.

Do I have lifetime access?

Yes! Once enrolled, you have lifetime access to course materials.

Need more help?

Email us at support@learnhub.com or use the Contact form.
